# About us

Open to all faculty and grad students interested in computer science education. We meet every other week to discuss current research in the field. Each session, one member facilitates a discussion of a paper (or occasionally a talk), followed by open discussion.

At the end of each year, we form working groups that carry ideas forward into January.

The meeting time for the 2026W1 semester (September to December 2026) is on Fridays from 12 to 1PM.
