# Members

All are welcome to attend as frequently as you like! During reading group semesters, drop ins are welcome. It's harder to accommodate drop-ins once working groups start but if you are interested in joining a working group on a semi-regular basis, feel free to email the group of interest to work out an arrangement. 

To join the reading group mailing list, subscribe by emailing cs<code>[dash]</code>ed<code>[dash]</code>rg<code>[dash]</code>request<code>[at]</code>cs.ubc.ca with "join" in the subject or body. Please be sure to replace <code>[dash]</code> with <code>-</code> and [at] with the appropriate symbol.
